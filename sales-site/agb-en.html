Terms

Sample · Private Preview
This example assumes invoiced sales to commercial hosts. The preview is for demonstrating the product. You cannot enter into a contract through it.

1. Scope

These sample terms cover Gastemappe24 Pro for commercial operators of hotels, guesthouses and holiday rentals. The contracting party's details will be added to the Legal Notice.

2. Scope of service

Gastemappe24 Pro provides a digital guest guide for the property specified in the offer. Hosts can update Wi-Fi information, arrival instructions and recommendations and share them with guests by link or QR code. The individual offer defines the agreed scope.

3. Ordering and payment

In this example, prospective customers receive an individual offer on request. A contract is formed when they accept it. Payment is by invoice. The offer specifies the payment deadline and activation date.

4. Licence, hosting and costs

In this example the one-time licence fee is €149 excluding VAT. Hosting and updates are included for twelve months from activation. Continued hosting costs €19 per year from the second year.

In this example, hosting is renewed for one year when the customer confirms the renewal. Without confirmation, hosting ends when the paid period expires. The hosted guide is then unavailable via its previous link and QR code.

5. Content and access

Customers maintain their own content and ensure they have the necessary rights to published text and images. They must keep their admin credentials confidential and notify the provider of suspected misuse.

6. Service issues and statutory rights

Service issues can be reported via the contact details in the Legal Notice. Statutory provisions apply to defects and liability.
