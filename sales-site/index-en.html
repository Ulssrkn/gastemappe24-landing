GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Do my guests need an app?

The guest guide opens directly in the browser. Guests can scan the QR code or open the link.

Can I update my content later?

Yes. Update your content in the editor. Guests see the latest information using the same link or QR code.

Which languages does the guest guide support?

German, English, Turkish, French, Spanish, Croatian, Albanian, Swedish, Norwegian and Italian.

What does the guest guide cost from the second year?

The one-time licence fee is €149 excluding VAT. The first year of hosting and updates is included. Maintenance and hosting cost €19 per year from the second year.

Where can I try the guest view?

In the Live demo you can open a sample hotel's guest guide.
