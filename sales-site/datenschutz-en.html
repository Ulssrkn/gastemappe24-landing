2. Visiting the site and hosting

When you visit, the hosting service receives technical connection data such as the IP address and requested page to deliver the website. Depending on its configuration, it may log access data for troubleshooting and service security.

The hosting provider, recipients, legal basis, retention period and any transfers to third countries will be specified once the hosting setup is final.

3. Contact and orders

The private preview has no contact form or ordering feature. If enquiries and orders are later handled by email, this typically involves names, contact details and message content. The final notice will describe the actual process, legal bases and retention periods.

4. Cookies and audience measurement

The pages themselves set no cookies and load no audience analytics. The hosting service manages access to the private preview. Its access technology and any services added later will be described in the final notice.

5. External live demo

The live demo is provided as a link. Clicking it opens a separate website. Data processing in the demo, admin area and guest guides will be described separately for those services.

6. Your rights

Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data and object to certain processing. You can withdraw consent for future processing. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
